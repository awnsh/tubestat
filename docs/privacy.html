Privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026. Applies to the TubeStat browser extension and this website.

TubeStat keeps a record of your YouTube viewing on your own device. It has no server and no account. Your viewing history is never sent to the developer or anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising.

What TubeStat records

Only while you use youtube.com in a browser where TubeStat is installed:

  • Videos you play: the video’s ID, title, channel name and channel ID, length, YouTube’s category for it, whether it’s a Short or a live stream, when you watched, how long it actually played, and how much of it you covered.
  • Channels you visit: name, handle, profile picture address, and public subscriber count, recorded at most once a day.
  • Your YouTube history page, when you open it: for each video listed on youtube.com/feed/history (which includes what you watched on your phone and other signed-in devices), the same details as above where YouTube shows them, the day it’s listed under, and how far YouTube’s progress bar reaches. You can turn this off in Profile.
  • Your subscriptions: which channels you’re subscribed to, read from YouTube pages you open, and the date you subscribed when YouTube makes it available.
  • Your settings, and any friend groups and stat codes you add.

TubeStat does not record your searches, comments, likes, recommendations, messages, account details, or anything you do on any other website.

Where it’s stored

Everything is kept in your browser’s extension storage on this device. If you choose to sign in with Google, a copy is also kept in a private app folder in your own Google Drive (Google’s “app data” folder), so your other devices can share it. Only TubeStat can open that folder, it doesn’t appear in your Drive files, and the developer has no access to it. TubeStat has no server of its own. Uninstalling TubeStat deletes this device’s copy; “Delete everything” in Profile deletes both.

What leaves your device

Your viewing data never does. TubeStat makes only these network requests:

  • Images from YouTube’s servers (video thumbnails and channel pictures), to display them in your dashboard and draw share cards.
  • The Internet Archive (web.archive.org). To estimate how many subscribers a channel had when you subscribed, TubeStat asks the Wayback Machine for saved copies of that channel’s public page around your subscription date. Each request contains one channel’s ID or @handle and a date, and nothing about you. This only happens for subscriptions with a known date, which needs Google sign-in. You can turn it off in Profile → “Their size when you subscribed”.
  • GitHub’s public release page, at most twice a day, to see whether a newer version of TubeStat is out. It’s a plain request for the latest version number and sends nothing about you. Copies installed from the Chrome Web Store skip it, and you can turn it off in Profile.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in. This is off by default. Signing in asks Google for three things: your email address and name (to show which account is signed in), read-only access to your YouTube subscriptions (to learn when you subscribed, and those channels’ public statistics), and TubeStat’s own app folder in your Google Drive (to keep your stats there). TubeStat then syncs your stats with that folder about every half hour and reads your subscriptions about once a week. It can’t see or change any other file in your Drive, and can’t change anything on your YouTube account. Access tokens are kept in memory only and never saved. Signing out stops all of this; you can also remove TubeStat’s access in your Google account settings at any time. TubeStat’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What friends see

Nothing, unless you share something yourself. A stat code contains your chosen name and this week’s totals (watch time, number of videos, number of creators, and streaks). It never contains video titles, channels, or history. Share cards are images created on your device that you download or copy yourself.

Your choices

  • Pause recording at any time under Tracking on the Profile page.
  • Export everything TubeStat has recorded as a JSON file under Your data on the Profile page.
  • Delete all of it with Delete everything on the Profile page, or by removing the extension.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. It has no analytics, cookies, or tracking of its own. GitHub and Google may log requests as described in their own privacy policies.

Children

TubeStat isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect information about anyone. All data stays on the device where it was recorded.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or concerns: open an issue on the TubeStat GitHub repository.
